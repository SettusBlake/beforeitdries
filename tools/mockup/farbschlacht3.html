    How the duel works
    • Your three blots stand on the sheet. Each turn one of them moves, then acts. A blot that just acted is drying (blue drop) and rests for one turn. Tap a rival blot to read it.
    • Paint is ground. Walking on free paper or your own paint costs 1, rival paint costs 2, mud stops you. Every blueprint moves its own way (see its card).
    • Dry and Wet stamp the blueprint's shape within reach, as before: dry paints over dried paint, wet bleeds into free paper but slides off dry paint, complementary wet colours turn to mud.
    • Splash hits a rival blot in reach: Saturation plus a d20 against its Grain plus a d20. Damage takes away Opacity, and the blot fades on the sheet. Complementary colours hit for 1.5 times and stain (2 more damage for two rounds). Harmonious colours hit for 0.75.
    • Standing on your own wet paint: +1 reach for splashes and +1 opacity each round. Standing in a rival's complementary wet colour costs 2 each round.
    • Soak heals (most on your own wet paint), rinses stains and gives one water.
    • Every blueprint has an ability. Each action charges it by 20 plus the blot's Glow (gold ring). Standing on your own wet paint adds 10 each round. When the ring is full, the ability button lights up.
    • Traits from the blot's own painting work in the duel: salt crits more, alcohol dodges, blooms add a perk, a fingerprint charges twice as fast, phthalo stains, opera fades but hits harder, granulating paint is harder to cover.
    • A blot with a second pigment can paint and splash in that colour too (one weaker), useful when its main colour is harmonious to the target.
    • Every splash can be dodged (Flow) and can crit (a natural 20, more with salt).
    • After rounds 3 and 6 the Eraser wipes a row and grazes any blot in it. Lightfastness can save paint and blots.
    • Win by washing out all three rival blots, or by having more cells when the paper dries after 8 rounds. Each blot still standing adds 3.
    • Rival strength: the Apprentice makes loose choices and starts with one water, the Journeyman picks well and starts with two, the Master plays its best move and starts with three like you.
    Every blot is unique, every duel too.
