How to play

  • Every blot is a die. Its sides come from the blot itself: stars (★ with its level) and symbols from its stats. Flow gives Step, Opacity gives Brush, Saturation gives Splash, Grain gives Guard, Glow gives Glow. Tap ⓘ on a die to see its net.
  • Opening: both sides unfold one blot for free next to their palette.
  • Each turn pick three of your six dice and roll them. Symbols go to your pool and keep.
  • Stars summon: a blot can come out if the other dice show stars worth at least its level. Its die unfolds onto the sheet as six cells of wet paint, touching your own paint. At most three blots stand on the sheet.
  • Then each blot may move once (1 Step) and act once: Dry stamp (1 Brush), Wet stamp (1 Brush and 1 water), Splash (1 Splash), its ability (2 Glow) or Soak (free: heals, +1 water).
  • Blots only travel through paint. Bare paper is a wall, except for flyers and hoppers. Own paint costs 1, rival paint 2, mud stops you.
  • Splash: Saturation plus a d20 against Grain plus a d20. Complementary colours hit for 1.5 times and stain, harmonious ones for 0.75. A Guard in your pool halves a hit by itself.
  • Palettes: each side has three hearts. Stand next to the rival palette and splash it: one heart, every time. Break all three and you win.
  • Washed out blots lose their die for good, so you roll with fewer.
  • The Eraser wipes a row after rounds 3 and 6. After 8 rounds the paper dries: cells plus 3 per heart left.
