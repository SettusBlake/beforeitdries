    How the paint behaves
    • Two painters, one sheet. Cover more paper than your rival before it dries.
    • Each of your three blots is a brush. Its blueprint is its stamp, shown as the little grid.
    • Tap the paper to aim, tap again (or Paint) to paint. Outlines show what happens: green free, amber contested with your odds, brown turns to mud, grey no effect.
    • Dry brush is precise and can paint over dried paint: your Opacity plus a d20 against their Grain plus a d20.
    • Wet costs one water. The paint stays wet for two rounds and bleeds into free paper (Flow), but it slides off dried paint.
    • Painting into wet paint: Saturation plus a d20 decides. Complementary colours cancel out and turn to mud, which counts for nobody. Each brush card says which rival colours it muddies.
    • A brush you just used is drying and rests for one turn.
    • After rounds 3 and 6 the Eraser wipes a row. Lightfastness can save your paint.
    • After 8 rounds the paper is dry. More cells wins.
    Every blot is unique, every duel too.
